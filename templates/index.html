Under the Hood

A transparent look at the enterprise-grade tools powering your custom infrastructure.

Python & Flask (Backend Architecture)

A lightning-fast, lightweight microframework that allows us to write highly customized business logic without the bloat of massive CMS platforms.

PostgreSQL (Relational Database)

The world's most advanced open-source relational database. We use it to ensure strict data integrity, allowing complex querying between your clients, appointments, and logistics.

MinIO / S3 API (Object Storage)

High-performance, private object storage. Instead of storing your sensitive client documents in a public web folder, they are secured in an isolated bucket architecture.

Fernet Symmetric Encryption

All internal communications between you and your clients in the portal are scrambled using url-safe base64-encoded 32-byte keys, ensuring absolute privacy.

Stripe API (Payment Gateway)

PCI-compliant payment processing directly integrated into your database to handle complex quotes, dynamic estimating, and subscription billing seamlessly.
